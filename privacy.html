Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026

In short: Scrollbane has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of its own. Your app usage and your list of apps never leave your phone. The only things that go online are Google's optional rewarded ads and Google Play's purchase of Scrollbane Premium, described below.

This policy covers the Scrollbane app for Android ("Scrollbane", "the app", "we"). It says what the app reads, where that information goes, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

Scrollbane needs some of your phone's information to work. All of it is used only by the app, on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and none of it is ever used for ads.

InformationWhy the app reads it
App usage history (which app is in front and for how long, from Android's Usage Access)To notice when one of the apps you picked opens, so the Monster Ambush can appear, and to count your time away and the time you reclaim. Scrollbane never sees what you do inside an app.
The list of apps you can launchSo you can pick which apps to guard. Only the apps you pick are saved.
Drawing over other apps (the overlay permission)Only to show the Monster Ambush on top of an app you picked, once your free time for the day is spent. Nothing is read from the screen.
Your game save (your hero, items, progress, settings, the apps you guard and your daily time away)To play the game. It's stored in the app's private storage.
NotificationsCreated on the phone (for example, your evening tally). They're never sent from a server.

Your phone's own backup

If you've turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android includes your Scrollbane save in it, so your progress comes back on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. We can't see or reach it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Scrollbane can offer an optional ad: when you're out of Loot Chests or keys, you may choose to watch one for a small reward, at most a few times a day. Ads are never shown on their own, never on the Monster Ambush, and never required. Players with Scrollbane Premium see no ads.

The ads come from Google's Mobile Ads SDK. It doesn't start when the app opens: it starts, and loads one ad in advance, the first time in a session that an ad could be offered (the Vault with no chests left, or when you run out of keys). Once it runs, Google may collect and process, under its own policies:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can give an approximate location
  • How you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or watched to the end)
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the SDK

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. It's encrypted in transit. Scrollbane never gives the ads SDK your app usage, your app list or your game save.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested, and Settings has Ad privacy options so you can change your answer at any time.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path depends on your phone). Scrollbane Premium turns the ads SDK off entirely. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (at most PG).

Scrollbane Premium (Google Play Billing)

Scrollbane Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google Play account owns Premium, so it can turn it on, restore it on a new phone, and remove it if a purchase is refunded. Google Play's handling of your purchase is covered by Google's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.
  • We never sell or share your information. We never receive your app usage, app list or save at all.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone, camera or precise location.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your save stays on your phone until you remove it. To delete everything Scrollbane stores, open your phone's Settings > Apps > Scrollbane > Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google's ads SDK or Google Play is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it at your Google Account.

Children

Scrollbane is meant for players 13 and older and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Your save is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Anything the ads SDK or Google Play sends travels encrypted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts sending something new off your phone, this page and the app's own permission screens will say so first.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on this site's page, or use the developer contact email on Scrollbane's Google Play listing.
